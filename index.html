FAQ

Do you issue the SOC 2 certificate / report?

No. Only a licensed CPA (or equivalent under AICPA rules) issues a SOC 2 report. Evidpack helps you prepare policies and evidence and export a pack for that engagement.

Is this a full GRC platform?

No. MVP is checklist + policy drafts + evidence vault + reminders + export.

Type I or Type II?

Built first for Type I (design of controls at a point in time). Type II is a longer journey; a thin MVP does not finish that alone.

Will this guarantee we pass the audit?

No tool can. We help you organize what auditors typically ask for. Your auditor still examines and opines.

Where is data hosted?

Abroad (non-CN production host). Regions and subprocessors will be listed on the trust page as we ship.
